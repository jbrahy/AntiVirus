§ 01 — Why open source

Protected, not monitored

Every other antivirus vendor asks you to trust a black box running on your machine, watching your files, and reporting who knows what back to who knows where. No one lets you check. NexGuard does.

NexGuard Labs built avtool, our scanner, from the ground up — and we publish every line of it. You don't have to take our word for what it does; you or anyone else can read it, build it yourself, or watch it in a network monitor while it runs. The only outbound call it ever makes is a plain GET to pull down the hash list. It never uploads a file, a filename, or anything about what's on your machine — there's no telemetry to disable because there's no telemetry to write. We open-sourced the scanner specifically so that promise isn't just something we say.

§ 02 — How it works

The ledger, not a guess

avtool, NexGuard's scanner, computes the SHA-256 fingerprint of every file you point it at, then checks that fingerprint against a hash database that NexGuard Labs curates and maintains. A match means the file is byte-identical to a known-bad sample. No match means exactly that: no match, not a clean bill of health.

You can also run avtool watch to keep an eye on a folder in real time — your Downloads, say — and review anything it catches with avtool review.

§ 03 — What this is, what it isn't

We'd rather undersell this

Most security tools promise more than a hash check can deliver. NexGuard doesn't.

NexGuard does

  • Catch files that exactly match a known-malicious hash
  • Stay open source — read every line at the link above
  • Sync a curated threat feed on your schedule
  • Pair cleanly alongside whatever you already run
  • Report to you, and only to you

NexGuard doesn't

  • Use heuristics or behavioral detection
  • Catch malware modified even slightly from a known sample
  • Replace a full antivirus suite
  • Promise anything a hash match can't back up
